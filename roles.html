Roles & cadence

Who owns what

The framework runs on five roles across each round. Parm is the framework lead — Jordan sets direction and owns budget; Ryan covers data and creative; Leo handles trafficking. Each role has a clear trigger and a clear deliverable — no diffused ownership, no waiting on email.

Jordan: this page is a first-draft allocation — edit as needed in the repo (site/roles.html). Swap names, adjust scope, add anyone I’ve missed. Handoff timing is opinionated but adjustable.

The five roles

Parm — Framework lead

Owns: the system itself. Round cadence, competitor scan, graduate calls, escalations, legal coordination, go-live approval.

Each round:

  • Runs the competitor scan at round kickoff (45–60 min).
  • Reviews the 4 proposed angles, picks 3 live + 1 bench.
  • Approves the briefs before they go to creative.
  • Reviews messaging on the populated templates; works with legal on copy flags (price claims, “updates included” wording, UGC/testimonial consent).
  • Gives final go-live approval once legal is clear and assets are Smartly-ready.
  • Watches the tracker daily once ads are live. Calls Gate 1 culls and Gate 2 graduations.
  • Writes the round recap in the Decision Log tab.
  • Flags anything off-baseline to leadership (unexpected wins, baseline shifts, budget re-asks).

Jordan — Paid media owner

Owns: strategic direction, budget, exec comms. Sponsors the framework; doesn’t run the round-by-round.

Each round:

  • Sets the round’s audience and win metric at kickoff.
  • Signs off on the budget envelope (per-approach €1.5k–€5k spend ceiling).
  • Receives the round recap from Parm; handles any exec-level comms.
  • Escalation point for budget re-asks, cross-team conflicts, and strategic pivots.

Ryan — Data & analysis

Owns: the tracker. Gate-readiness signals. Round-level data integrity.

Each round:

  • Verifies Meta data is flowing into the tracker (Ad Tests tab populated daily).
  • Monitors Gate 1 triggers: flags any approach where CPL > €3 past €500 spent.
  • Monitors Gate 2 triggers: Bayesian prob-to-be-best, spend-to-ceiling, frequency.
  • Posts a short Slack/Teams update when a gate threshold is crossed.
  • Validates the round recap before it’s finalised.
  • Maintains Post-Grad Watch tab for locked winners (ongoing CPA drift alerts).

Ryan — Creative & copy

Owns: the ads themselves. Brief → template → delivery.

Each round:

  • Takes approved briefs and populates the three HTML templates.
  • Drafts headline options per approach (picks one to ship, holds 2–3 for iteration).
  • Sources or commissions imagery (plugin UI shots, producer photography, comparison tables).
  • Files a copy doc with flags for Parm to review (price claims, “updates included” wording, testimonial consent).
  • Hands approved assets to Leo in Smartly-ready format (4:5, 1:1, 9:16 per approach) once Parm has signed off.

Leo — Media & trafficking

Owns: getting the ads live, pacing them, and keeping delivery clean.

Each round:

  • Sets up the three approach ad sets in Meta Ads Manager via Smartly.
  • Enforces equal budget split and spend parity (within 10% across the three).
  • QA on placement rendering across feed / Stories / Reels before launch.
  • Pauses losers when Gate 1 triggers (per Parm’s call).
  • Moves graduates into the CPA campaign and ships replacements within 48h of Gate 2 call.
  • Owns naming convention: NI_NI360_R[N]_[A|B|C]_[format].

Weekly cadence

Monday — round health

Ryan posts gate-readiness summary (CPL by approach, spend-to-ceiling, frequency). Parm flags any calls to be made that week.

Mid-round — gate watch

Ryan posts daily when any gate threshold is within 20% of tripping. No posts if nothing’s moving.

Round end — recap

Parm posts a one-paragraph recap: which approaches graduated, why (hypothesis), what’s next, what’s on the bench. Link to the Decision Log entry. Jordan gets the recap for exec comms.

Escalation

Anything that breaks the framework’s assumptions goes to Parm first (framework lead). Parm escalates to Jordan for budget, strategic direction, or exec comms.

  • Baseline CPA shifts > 20% — the current round’s comparisons may be invalid.
  • All three approaches trip Gate 1 — the audience, not the creative, is the issue.
  • A locked winner’s CPA drifts > 15% from its winning round — probably fatigue; trigger a variant round.
  • A competitor launches a look-alike of one of our live approaches — decide whether to pivot, double down, or hold.
  • Legal flags on copy — pause the affected ad, loop in legal, replace or re-word before re-shipping.
