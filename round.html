Stage 3

Scan findings

Territory claimed by competitors · territory open for us

Claimed by competitors 5

  • Sound packs & collab Splice owns this hard. Influencer + producer-collab content. Don’t fight here.
  • AI mastering LANDR is synonymous with it. Their ads lean “upload & we master” convenience.
  • Cinematic / TV scoring Output dominates the cinematic instrument category with Arcade and Substance.
  • “Try it free” trial CTA All three competitors lead with a trial offer; it’s the standard CTA, not a differentiator.
  • Generic creator testimonials Lots of unnamed-creator quotes. None reference specific records or credits.

Territory open for us 4

  • Pro-grade plugin credibility — A None of the three claim “the actual tools pros use on records.” Specificity wins here.
  • Outright vs subscription comparison — B Nobody is showing the outright vs sub price math. Komplete is ~$1,599 outright; framing 360 against that price anchor is wide open.
  • Named producer + verified credits — C Generic creator quotes are everywhere; verified credits are not. Trustpilot rating + named producer is a clean differentiator.
  • “All future updates included” framing Bench angle — LANDR mentions updates weakly; nobody leads with it. Holds for Round 2.
Findings here are seeded from Round 1’s scan. Parm updates this list after each round’s scan; canonical version lives in the tracker’s Decision Log tab.
