The framework in one paragraph

Every round we scan competitors, propose 4 distinct positioning angles, pick 3 to go live (the 4th waits on the bench), run them with equal budget, and let the tracker decide when to call it — not the calendar. Any approach that clears the graduation bar — Bayesian prob-to-be-best ≥ 90%, or CPA at/below the €80 baseline with ≥ 40 trial starts — graduates into the CPA campaign. One, two, or all three approaches can graduate in a single round; the CPA campaign wants creative volume. Clear losers are retired. The bench angle fills the first open slot, fresh briefs fill the rest, and the next round starts on the next competitor scan.
