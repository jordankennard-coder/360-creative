Once an approach graduates into the CPA campaign, Ryan monitors its ongoing CPA in the tracker’s Post-Grad Watch tab. If CPA drifts > 15% from its graduating round’s CPA, it triggers a variant round (new headlines / new hero image / CTA swaps) rather than a full framework restart.
