Locked winners

Per the playbook: after three consecutive rounds where the same approach wins, we stop rotating that audience. The locked winner moves here, and we shift to within-approach creative variants, audience expansion, or placement optimization.

Post-Grad Watch

Once an approach is locked, Ryan monitors its ongoing CPA in the tracker’s Post-Grad Watch tab. If CPA drifts > 15% from the winning round’s CPA, it triggers a variant round (new headlines / new hero image / CTA swaps) rather than a full framework restart.
